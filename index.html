Compare with actual revenue and silo cost

Silo cost is the October Total Dept Cost from the KPI Tracker: each person's monthly pay x their % in the silo, plus the silo's software and other monthly costs. Profit = revenue minus silo cost.

How it's worked out

  • People and % allocated by silo come from the October 2026 KPI Tracker (Roster & Allocation).
  • Working hours: a 40-hour week is 1,928 hours a year after 9 holidays and 2 weeks of vacation (160.7 a month). A 32-hour week is 1,512 (126 a month). Billable hours are those times the role's target.
  • Daljit counts as a CAM. Mandy, Zee, Michelle and Shilpa keep their own monthly hours.
  • October hours are client-tagged timesheet hours from Payroll Splits 2026, the same hours used in the October KPIs.
  • Actual revenue is the final October silo revenue from the KPI Tracker.
  • Silo cost is the final October Total Dept Cost from the KPI Tracker (pay x % allocated, plus software and other monthly costs). It isn't broken out by person here so individual pay stays private.
  • Profit at October hours value shows what the silo would make if every hour logged were billed at the role rate, minus the same cost.
  • Each person's hours are rounded to one decimal before multiplying, so every total adds up exactly from the rows.
